2. **[BrokenMath: A Benchmark for Sycophancy in Theorem Proving with LLMs](https://openreview.net/forum?id=Y5bEI4cuMd)**

3. **[What's In My Human Feedback? Learning Interpretable Descriptions of Preference Data](https://openreview.net/forum?id=sC6A1bFDUt)**
